## App Description

Planto is a plant care reminder app.
It helps you remember when to water your plants, how much water they need, where to place them, and how much sunlight each one needs.

## Features

- Add, edit, and delete plant reminders.
- Mark a plant as watered with a checkmark completed plants move to the bottom of the list.
- Progress bar showing how many plants you have watered today.
- Local notifications that remind you to water your plants.

## Technologies Used

- Swift and SwiftUI
- SwiftData
- UserNotifications
